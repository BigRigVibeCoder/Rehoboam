# AGENTS.md — briefing for any AI agent working on Rehoboam

> **A handoff is in progress (2026-10-18): read `HANDOFF.md` first.** It lists the open pull requests, the branches in flight, who owns what, and the next steps in order.

> **BLUF:** Rehoboam is a deterministic world engine, built by AI agents and governed by one human (the director) through CODEX. Read `CLAUDE.md` for the non-negotiables first. Then find your role and your sprint through the generated index. Never change a contract to make your code pass: propose an `EVO-` instead.

## Reading order (every session)
1. `CLAUDE.md`: the non-negotiables (determinism, three planes, single writer, Registry-only mechanics, licences) and the checks. **It binds every agent, whatever its vendor**; the name is historical (GOV-011 §1).
2. `CODEX/README.md`, then `CODEX/00_INDEX/MANIFEST.yaml`: generate it first with `python -I bin/gen_manifest.py` (it is not committed), then filter it; do not crawl the repo.
3. `CODEX/10_GOVERNANCE/GOV-007` (who decides what), `GOV-005` (the work loop), `GOV-002` (testing), **`GOV-009` (Git and GitHub: branches, commits, pull requests, merges), `GOV-010` (CI) and `GOV-011` (ownership, independence, records and handoffs between agents)**.
4. Your role: `CODEX/80_AGENTS/AGT-*`.
5. Your sprint: `CODEX/05_PROJECT/SPR-*`, and everything it names as binding.

## Commits, pull requests and merges (GOV-009)
- **Commits:** `type(SPR-NNN): T-NN description`, then a body with `Why:`, `What:`, `Refs:` and your own tool's attribution lines.
- **`main` moves only by pull request.** The Architect merges each PR with a merge commit, pinned to the head SHA that CI passed. Never push to `main`.
- **History is never rewritten.** Never force-push, and bring `main` into a branch by merging it, not by rebasing.
- **Generated files are never typed.** Baselines, goldens, locks and skeletons are regenerated by their tools.
- **Only the owner pushes to a branch.** The owner is named by the `Agent:` line in its PR body (GOV-011 §2).
