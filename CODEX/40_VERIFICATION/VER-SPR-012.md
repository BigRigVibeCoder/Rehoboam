## 5. The `solo` baseline (announced to the director)
`worlds/solo/baselines/solo_yearly_200y.ladder` is the first real golden world: one founder (Ada, female, born at tick −13,236,200, aged 25.5 at genesis) under `M2.demography`'s rung-0 slice.
```
rehoboam-ladder v1
world solo
seed 1
years 200
rung yearly
tuple engine_build=rh-golden-0.0.0-built:M2.demography registry_version=0.2.0
      manifest_hash=sha256:1f0c52a43606ad0befdcc0adfe23627d83400684af525f12832a77f00abcf8c6 seed=1
systems built M2.demography not_built M1.regions,M3.agriculture,M4.economy,S5.daily_life
```
- **The input.** One scope-only material input on Ada, at tick 27,786,607 (year 53, day 216, 06:07). That instant falls inside the month her death was drawn for, so the run takes the whole of ADR-0025 item 3: the epoch bump and the cancel-and-re-sample of a pending death.
- **Ada's death.** The re-sample misses, and she dies at tick 29,975,040 (year 57), drawn at the Month pulse of 29,937,600 at epoch 1. `EXTINCTION` comes at the same tick, and the run continues to year 200 with an empty ledger.
- **Regeneration.** The baseline was regenerated by `bin/regen_baseline.sh solo` in `bd2e718` `[C3]`.
- **Announcement.** It was announced to the director before the merge (2026-10-17).

## 6. Lessons
1. A golden run proves only the paths it takes. `solo`'s first input missed every pending death, so the re-sample path was outside the ladder, and only a differential oracle saw two mutants. An input in a golden script should be placed to exercise the path it claims; check it with a probe.
2. A statistical acceptance test needs a power statement. A per-seed band of 99.9% over 32 seeds is a 3% family-wise false alarm with little power against a 1% schedule error; a pooled statistic costs nothing.
3. A lint scoped to the crates that should not call something misses the names defined elsewhere. Scope it to every crate that could provide the name, or make the boundary structural (A-51).
