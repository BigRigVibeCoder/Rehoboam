# The annals of the line of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #5)
# From the chronicle of ashcombe; templates 0.3.0

## The twenty-first year (Year of Ash)
On day 4 of month 4, the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #11) and the second daughter of Hugh Thorne were married.

## The twenty-second year (Year of Ash)
On day 2 of month 2, the second daughter of Hugh Thorne bore the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #11) a son, p-3374, the first child of that mother.
On day 2 of month 2, following the birth of p-3374, the child was received into the house of the second daughter of Hugh Thorne, the mother.

## The twenty-fifth year (Year of Ash)
On day 3 of month 3, p-3374 died of natural causes.
