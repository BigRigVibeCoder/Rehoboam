# The annals of House Ashcombe
# From the chronicle of ashcombe; templates 0.3.0

## Before the chronicle began
House Ashcombe entered the chronicle with four living members.
Founder #6, born on day 1 of month 1 of the sixtieth year before the epoch (Year of Ash), died on day 1 of month 1 of the twelfth year before the epoch (Year of Ash), before the chronicle began.
Founder #6 held the lordship of the Manor of Ash from day 1 of month 1 of the fortieth year before the epoch (Year of Ash) until day 1 of month 1 of the twelfth year before the epoch (Year of Ash).
Edric Ashcombe (Founder #1) held the lordship of the Manor of Ash from day 1 of month 1 of the twelfth year before the epoch (Year of Ash) as the chronicle opened.

## The second year (Year of Ash)
On day 5 of month 2, the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8) and the first daughter of Hugh Thorne were married.
On day 20 of month 11, the first daughter of Hugh Thorne bore the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8) a son, p-aae0, the first child of that mother.

## The fourth year (Year of Ash)
On day 9 of month 4, Maud bore Edric Ashcombe (Founder #1) a daughter, the second daughter of Edric Ashcombe (p-997c), the third child of that mother.

## The eighth year (Year of Ash)
On day 20 of month 2, Edric Ashcombe (Founder #1) died of natural causes.
On day 20 of month 2, after the death of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #1), the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8) succeeded Edric Ashcombe (Founder #1).

## The ninth year (Year of Ash)
On day 7 of month 5, Hugh Thorne declared a feud against the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8) out of jealousy of the succession of the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8).

## The tenth year (Year of Ash)
On day 13 of month 1, the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8) was murdered by Hugh Thorne.
On day 13 of month 1, after the death of the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8), p-aae0 succeeded the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8).
On day 13 of month 1, the first daughter of Hugh Thorne became regent for p-aae0, who was under age.
On day 13 of month 1, with the death of the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8), the feud between the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8) and Hugh Thorne ended.
On day 1 of month 9, the first daughter of Hugh Thorne bore the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8) a son, p-e400, the second child of that mother.

## The thirteenth year (Year of Ash)
On day 9 of month 7, the second son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #9) declared a feud against Hugh Thorne, moved by the death of the first son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #8).

## The fourteenth year (Year of Ash)
On day 2 of month 2, the second son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #9) and Hugh Thorne were reconciled, and their feud ended.

## The fifteenth year (Year of Ash)
On day 6 of month 6, the first son of Hugh Thorne declared a feud against the second son of Edric Ashcombe (Founder #9).

## The eighteenth year (Year of Ash)
On day 20 of month 11, the regency of the first daughter of Hugh Thorne for p-aae0 ended.

## The twenty-third year (Year of Ash)
On day 2 of month 3, p-aae0 was deposed.
On day 2 of month 3, after the deposition of p-aae0, p-e400 succeeded p-aae0.
On day 2 of month 3, p-aae0 gained a claim against p-e400.
On day 2 of month 3, the first daughter of Hugh Thorne became regent for p-e400, who was under age.

## The twenty-fourth year (Year of Ash)
On day 9 of month 1, p-aae0 pressed a claim against p-e400.

## The twenty-sixth year (Year of Ash)
On day 1 of month 9, the regency of the first daughter of Hugh Thorne for p-e400 ended.

## The twenty-seventh year (Year of Ash)
On day 5 of month 5, p-e400 was deposed, p-aae0 standing as the leading claimant.
On day 5 of month 5, after the deposition of p-e400, p-aae0 succeeded p-e400.
On day 5 of month 5, p-e400 gained a claim against p-aae0.

## The twenty-eighth year (Year of Ash)
On day 3 of month 3, p-e400 renounced a claim.

## The thirty-fifth year (Year of Ash)
On day 1 of month 1, after the interregnum, p-aae0 succeeded the first son of Hugh Thorne.

## The thirty-sixth year (Year of Ash)
On day 2 of month 2, p-aae0 granted a seat to p-e400.
