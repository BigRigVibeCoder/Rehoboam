# The annals of House Ashcombe
# From the chronicle of ashcombe; templates 0.1.0

## Before the chronicle began
House Ashcombe entered the chronicle with 2 living members.
Person #1001 of House Ashcombe, born on day 1 of month 1 of the 60th year before the epoch (Year of Ash), died on day 1 of month 1 of the twelfth year before the epoch (Year of Ash), before the chronicle began.
Person #1001 of House Ashcombe held the lordship of the Manor of Ash from day 1 of month 1 of the 40th year before the epoch (Year of Ash) until day 1 of month 1 of the twelfth year before the epoch (Year of Ash).
Edric Ashcombe has held the lordship of the Manor of Ash since day 1 of month 1 of the twelfth year before the epoch (Year of Ash).

## The first year (Year of Ash)
On day 14 of month 3, Maud bore a daughter, the second child of Maud.

## The third year (Year of Ash)
On day 1 of month 6, a son of Edric Ashcombe and a daughter of Hugh Thorne were married.
