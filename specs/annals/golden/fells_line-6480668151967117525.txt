# The annals of the line of Brannoc Fell
# From the chronicle of fells; templates 0.4.0

## The second year (Year of the Fells)
On day 10 of month 3, Brannoc Fell died of natural causes.
On day 10 of month 3, after the death of Brannoc Fell, the succession to a title found no clear heir, and an interregnum began.
On day 1 of month 6, after the interregnum, the first son of Brannoc Fell succeeded Brannoc Fell.
On day 1 of month 6, after the interregnum, the first son of Brannoc Fell succeeded Ulric Carrow.
