# The annals of House Carrow
# From the chronicle of fells; templates 0.4.0

## Before the chronicle began
House Carrow entered the chronicle with two living members.
Founder #7, born on day 5 of month 5 of the seventieth year before the epoch (Year of the Fells), died on day 3 of month 3 of the fifteenth year before the epoch (Year of the Fells), before the chronicle began.
Founder #7 held the seat of Carrow from day 5 of month 5 of the fifty-fourth year before the epoch (Year of the Fells) until day 3 of month 3 of the fifteenth year before the epoch (Year of the Fells).
Ulric Carrow held the seat of Carrow from day 3 of month 3 of the fifteenth year before the epoch (Year of the Fells) as the chronicle opened.

## The second year (Year of the Fells)
On day 4 of month 4, Ulric Carrow died of natural causes.
On day 4 of month 4, after the death of Ulric Carrow, the succession to a title found no clear heir, and an interregnum began.
On day 1 of month 6, after the interregnum, the first son of Brannoc Fell succeeded Ulric Carrow.
