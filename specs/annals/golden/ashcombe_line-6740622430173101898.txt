# The annals of the line of Edric Ashcombe
# From the chronicle of ashcombe; templates 0.2.0

## The second year (Year of Ash)
On day 5 of month 2, the first son of Edric Ashcombe and the first daughter of Hugh Thorne were married.
On day 20 of month 11, the first daughter of Hugh Thorne bore the first son of Edric Ashcombe a son, p-aae0, the first child of that mother.

## The fourth year (Year of Ash)
On day 9 of month 4, Maud bore Edric Ashcombe a daughter, the first daughter of Edric Ashcombe, the third child of that mother.

## The eighth year (Year of Ash)
On day 20 of month 2, Edric Ashcombe died of natural causes.
On day 20 of month 2, after the death of Edric Ashcombe, the first son of Edric Ashcombe succeeded Edric Ashcombe.

## The ninth year (Year of Ash)
On day 7 of month 5, Hugh Thorne declared a feud against the first son of Edric Ashcombe out of jealousy of the succession of the first son of Edric Ashcombe.

## The tenth year (Year of Ash)
On day 13 of month 1, the first son of Edric Ashcombe was murdered by Hugh Thorne.
On day 13 of month 1, after the death of the first son of Edric Ashcombe, p-aae0 succeeded the first son of Edric Ashcombe.
On day 13 of month 1, the first daughter of Hugh Thorne became regent for p-aae0, who was under age.
On day 13 of month 1, with the death of the first son of Edric Ashcombe, the feud between the first son of Edric Ashcombe and Hugh Thorne ended.
On day 1 of month 9, the first daughter of Hugh Thorne bore the first son of Edric Ashcombe a son, p-e400, the second child of that mother.

## The thirteenth year (Year of Ash)
On day 9 of month 7, the second son of Edric Ashcombe declared a feud against Hugh Thorne, moved by the death of the first son of Edric Ashcombe.

## The fourteenth year (Year of Ash)
On day 2 of month 2, the second son of Edric Ashcombe and Hugh Thorne were reconciled, and their feud ended.

## The eighteenth year (Year of Ash)
On day 20 of month 11, the regency of the first daughter of Hugh Thorne for p-aae0 ended.

## The twenty-third year (Year of Ash)
On day 1 of month 3, p-aae0 was deposed.
On day 1 of month 3, after the deposition of p-aae0, p-e400 succeeded p-aae0.
On day 1 of month 3, p-aae0 gained a claim against p-e400.
On day 1 of month 3, the first daughter of Hugh Thorne became regent for p-e400, who was under age.

## The twenty-fourth year (Year of Ash)
On day 9 of month 1, p-aae0 pressed a claim against p-e400.

## The twenty-sixth year (Year of Ash)
On day 1 of month 9, the regency of the first daughter of Hugh Thorne for p-e400 ended.

## The twenty-seventh year (Year of Ash)
On day 5 of month 5, p-e400 was deposed, p-aae0 standing as the leading claimant.
On day 5 of month 5, after the deposition of p-e400, p-aae0 succeeded p-e400.
On day 5 of month 5, p-e400 gained a claim against p-aae0.

## The twenty-eighth year (Year of Ash)
On day 3 of month 3, p-e400 renounced a claim.

## The thirty-fourth year (Year of Ash)
On day 6 of month 6, p-aae0 gained a claim to a vacant title.

## The thirty-fifth year (Year of Ash)
On day 1 of month 1, after the interregnum, p-aae0 succeeded the first son of Hugh Thorne.

## The thirty-sixth year (Year of Ash)
On day 2 of month 2, p-aae0 granted a seat to p-e400.
