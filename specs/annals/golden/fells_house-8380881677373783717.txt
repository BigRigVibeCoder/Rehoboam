# The annals of House Fell
# From the chronicle of fells; templates 0.4.0

## Before the chronicle began
House Fell entered the chronicle with two living members.
Founder #3, born on day 2 of month 2 of the seventy-fifth year before the epoch (Year of the Fells), died on day 9 of month 9 of the twentieth year before the epoch (Year of the Fells), before the chronicle began.
Founder #3 held the chieftaincy of the High Fells from day 2 of month 2 of the fifty-ninth year before the epoch (Year of the Fells) until day 9 of month 9 of the twentieth year before the epoch (Year of the Fells).
Brannoc Fell held the chieftaincy of the High Fells from day 9 of month 9 of the twentieth year before the epoch (Year of the Fells) as the chronicle opened.

## The second year (Year of the Fells)
On day 10 of month 3, Brannoc Fell died of natural causes.
On day 10 of month 3, after the death of Brannoc Fell, the succession to a title found no clear heir, and an interregnum began.
On day 1 of month 6, after the interregnum, the first son of Brannoc Fell succeeded Brannoc Fell.
On day 1 of month 6, after the interregnum, the first son of Brannoc Fell succeeded Ulric Carrow.
