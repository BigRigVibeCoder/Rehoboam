# 10 — Risk Register

*Status: Draft v1.1 (2026-10-15). Scale: Likelihood (L) and Impact (I) are each rated 1–5, and Score = L × I. Review the register at every phase gate. Each risk carries an **early-warning indicator** (EWI), the trigger that surfaces it before it bites, and an **owner** (roles as in `13` §1) who watches the EWI and starts the mitigation.*
*v1.1 changes: new risks R-23 (sifter flood), R-24 (knowledge volume), R-25 (review bottleneck), R-26 (plane leakage) and R-27 (1M throughput); an Owner column; mitigations that relied on leases and decrees or on a single burn-in now use scheduled outcomes, the liveness gate and certification; Foresight and disk-growth indicators re-based on `08` v1.1; top-5 updated.*

## Top-5 focus (current)
1. **R-01**: prove story yield at G2, under the pre-registered protocol, before investing in 3D.
2. **R-02**: hold the sequencing line.
3. **R-25 / R-04**: your review hours are the scarcest resource. Keep agent work on auto-merge routes and keep the architecture rules mechanical.
4. **R-03 / R-26**: build the determinism harness and plane isolation first, in P0, and make DET-6 binding from P2.
5. **R-23 / R-05**: sifter yield (flood as much as drought) and calibration, both measured early.
