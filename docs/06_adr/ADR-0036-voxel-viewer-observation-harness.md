# ADR-0036: The voxel viewer, a native plane-B observation harness

**Status:** Accepted in principle (director, D51, 2026-10-17). This text is draft 0.1, for the director's and the red team's markup. · **Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Amends:** ADR-0035 (directive 5 and the "Cut" row of §5: a procedural voxel viewer is allowed as an observation harness), ADR-0030 (§7: a graphical observation harness is allowed before G2, off the critical path; text stays the G2 surface) · **Extends:** ADR-0024 (planes), ADR-0030 §3 and §6 (viewers read a buffer; street detail is hydration), ADR-0031 (fidelity by zoom) · **Plans:** `CODEX/05_PROJECT/PRJ-003_BevyVoxelViewer.md` and `SPR-033_BevyVoxelSpike.md` (the red team's; filed when they reach the repository) · **Spec:** `05.10` (the World Stream and the materializer), `03` R3D-2 and R3D-11, `09` V-R3D-13 and V-R3D-14

## Context

The red team audits what the engine produces: demographic emergence, settlement and route logic, dynastic drama. They have asked for a way to **see the world**, as their first observability requirement. Text and Parquet hide spatial anomalies. A household that lives on the wrong side of a river, or a route that ignores the terrain, is visible at a glance on a map and nearly invisible in 100,000 lines. The director agrees: the viewer is a testing and observability tool, not decoration (D51).

Two accepted ADRs stood in the way:
- **ADR-0035** cut every custom-3D work package (directive 5 and §5's "Cut" row). The reasons were the content cost (R-28) and a director who reads and listens rather than plays.
- **ADR-0030 §7** kept every view text-only until G2, so that effort goes to the thesis first.

Three things have changed since:
1. **Voxels remove most of the content cost.** A voxel world is generated by procedural grammars (terrain, plots, buildings by type and era) and needs no hand-made models, rigs or textures. That cost was the one that cut 3D.
2. **The stream already does the hard part.** Sealed segments, keyframes every 10 sim-years and the materializer (`05.10`; SPR-027) give the exact state at any tick. Rewind and fast-forward come free, and scrubbing never changes history.
3. **Bevy 0.20** (Rust, Apache-2.0 or MIT) links the repository's own crates directly: `rh-protocol`, the `rh-stream` reader, the materializer and `rh-worldgen`. The repository already pins `bevy_ecs` 0.20.

The Architect's analysis of 2026-10-17 compared four options: forking Veloren, forking Luanti, a web client on Three.js and WebGPU, and a native Bevy viewer. The red team agrees with its conclusion: no fork, and Bevy 0.20 (D51).

## Decision

### 1. What the viewer is
`rh-view` is a **native Bevy 0.20 application**. It reads the World Stream and the materializer, builds a deterministic voxel rendering of the world in plane B, and uses plane C to choose what to look at. It is a **harness** in ADR-0030's sense: a separate process with its own cursor, that never calls into the engine's simulation and never slows it.

| Plane | What the viewer does |
|---|---|
| **A** (authoritative) | **Reads only.** Live events from the stream's live edge (`A.sock`). Exact state at any tick from the materializer (nearest keyframe plus replay). It never writes plane A. |
| **B** (observation) | **Builds the voxel world here.** Terrain detail, plots, buildings, smoke, banners and idle movement are hydration (ADR-0030 §6; R3D-2). This detail is a deterministic function of (the state at the tick, the world seed, the Registry, the generator version). It never contradicts plane A: if channel A says the mill burned, it is burned. |
| **C** (narrative) | **Steers attention.** Sift matches and significance (`C.sock`) feed a camera director (R3D-7) and a "what happened here" panel. |

### 2. Boundaries (law, checked by lints)
1. **No engine crate depends on the viewer**, and the viewer adds no dependency to any engine crate. `bin/lint_arch.py` enforces both.
2. **The viewer lives in its own Cargo workspace** (`viewer/`, with its own `Cargo.lock`). It depends on the engine's crates by path, for reading only. Bevy's graph (`wgpu`, `winit`, image decoders) therefore never enters the engine's lock, the engine's `cargo deny` run, or V-INSP-05's deny-list scope. *(Architect's recommendation, 75%. The alternative is a member of the main workspace excluded from `default-members`, but `--workspace` gates would then compile Bevy on every PR.)*
3. **Inputs only through the control interface.** Any action the viewer offers is a journaled material input over `control.sock` (P2.7, D47), landing at the tip or at a scheduled future tick. An action at a past moment is refused unless issued as a fork (ADR-0025, ADR-0030 §4). The viewer has no other write path.
4. **Hydration is deterministic, and hashed.** Voxel content (which block is where) is integer and keyed: `H(world_seed, purpose, entity, tick-bucket, k)` with plane-B purposes registered like any other. Rendering (lighting, shading, frame timing) may use floats and the GPU. It is not hashed and need not be bit-identical across machines.
5. **Fidelity by zoom** (ADR-0031). Block-level detail only inside the camera's bubble; regions drawn from aggregates. The viewer never hydrates a whole large world.

### 3. Status of text
**Text stays the thesis surface.** G2 is rated on template-only text (ADR-0035 §8 item 3); the viewer is not a rating surface and is not on any gate's critical path. `rh-cli tail` and `rh-tui` remain the primary harnesses of P1 and P2.

### 4. Findings become tests
A visual finding is not closed until an automated check reproduces it: a lint, a property test, a band, or a sifting pattern. The viewer shows where to look, and the determinism and plausibility gates stay the evidence. This keeps the red team's findings in the CI record, and lets the viewer change without losing them.

### 5. What it can show, by phase
The viewer can show only what the simulation decides. Anything more is plane-B staging, labelled as such.

| What you see | Decided by | Available from |
|---|---|---|
| Terrain, regions, settlements, the route graph (at genesis) | M1 worldgen-lite | P1a (SPR-018) |
| Who lives where; births, deaths, marriages, successions, houses | M2, S3 F0, M7 | P1a |
| People moving through the day; routes in use | `S5.daily_life`, S3 F1 moves | P1b |
| Fields, harvests, stores, famine | M3, M4 | P1b |
| Armies, sieges | war systems | P4 |
| Building shapes, timbers, smoke, banners | nothing in plane A: plane-B generators | built with the viewer |

In P1a the geography is static: worldgen-lite fixes regions, settlements and routes at genesis, and S3 F0 has no moves. The first spike therefore shows demography and dynasty over a fixed landscape. **Route formation and movement anomalies become visible only with P1b's systems.**

### 6. Schedule (`11`, the viewer lane V.1–V.3)
- **Now:** this ADR. The red team iterates the look against recorded stream data, outside the engine's lanes.
- **After SPR-027** (the materializer, the pace command and `tail` filters; wave 5): AGT-002-TOOLS runs the read-only spike under PRJ-003, starting with SPR-033. It is a separate lane, off the G1a critical path.
  - Sprints follow GOV-005 §3: at most about 3 agent-days each. The red team's estimate of about 2 agent-weeks is split accordingly (PRJ-003).
  - Change class: C1, since the viewer is a tool and changes no hash. A change that touches an engine crate the viewer reads goes through that crate's own class and sprint.
- **With P1b's data:** movement, fields, mills, famine.
- **With P2.7's control channel:** the street-level inhabitant ("Gandalf"). Roaming as a spectator is a free camera and needs nothing. Being a person in the world means plane-A journaled inputs (`PlayerAction`, `SpeechAct`, origin `player`). How others react is decided by `T1.minds`; any spoken words are translated to speech acts under ADR-0034, never decided by an LLM.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| Fork Veloren (Rust, GPL-3.0) | Its server owns world state, NPC AI and physics. Two simulations would disagree, there is no rewind, and we would fight upstream indefinitely. The licence is allowed in personal-use mode; that was not the reason |
| Fork Luanti (C++ and Lua on an Irrlicht fork) | A mutable server map, no rewind, a Lua bridge, and a dated renderer |
| A web client (Three.js and WebGPU) first | A browser cannot read the local Unix sockets, so it needs a WebSocket bridge (`rh-server`, P2), a second stream decoder, and the materializer on a server or in WASM. It stays the red team's look-development sandbox, and Bevy can target WebGPU later |
| Keep text-only until G2 (ADR-0030 as written) | Leaves the red team blind to spatial anomalies for months, and the director has ruled it the first observability need (D51) |

## Consequences

- **A new lane.** One more Developer session after SPR-027. It competes for the 3–4 session budget and for usage, not for the one-C3-at-a-time merge slot, since it is C1.
- **CI cost.** Compiling Bevy is expensive. The viewer's CI job runs only when `viewer/` or a crate it reads changes, and runs headless: the hydration data path, the mesher and the determinism gates, with no GPU. Pixel tests are out of scope.
- **ADR-0035 directive 5 narrows.** "No custom 3D assets" still holds: no hand-made models, rigs or textures. Procedural voxel geometry in `rh-view` is allowed. The P5 2D diorama is unchanged.
- **R3D-2's generators arrive earlier**, in the viewer, scoped to what each phase simulates.
- **A second consumer of the materializer** makes V-STR-02's seek latency matter sooner. If interactive scrubbing is too slow, ADR-0030's "revisit" item (delta records for hot columns) comes forward.

## Requirements and tests
- **R3D-11** (`03`): the voxel viewer harness: a separate process, read-only on planes A and C, inputs only via `control.sock`, deterministic hydration, its own workspace.
- **V-R3D-13** (`09`): hydration determinism. The voxel content digest of a fixed bubble at a fixed tick is equal across two runs, 1 and N threads, and two builds. Reaching the tick by rewind and by forward play gives the same digest.
- **V-R3D-14** (`09`): isolation. No crate under `crates/` depends on `viewer/`; the engine's lock is unchanged by the viewer; V-PERF-09 holds with `rh-view` attached as one of the harnesses.

## Revisit when
- The spike cannot seek interactively (V-STR-02 latency): delta records for hot columns (ADR-0030).
- The viewer's CI cost exceeds its budget: move its job to the nightly.
- P5 begins: decide whether the 2D diorama remains a separate client or becomes a view mode of `rh-view`.
